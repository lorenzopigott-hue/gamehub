HOW TO PLAY

Start safely, learn the valley, then build your own dinosaur expedition.

1. MOVEWASD to walk. Mouse to look. Space to jump. Hold Shift to sprint.
2. SURVIVEGather berries, water, wood and stone. Stay fed and hydrated.
3. MEET DINOSPress E near a dinosaur. Herbivores need trust; predators need patience and food.
4. TAME & RIDEKeep feeding a wild companion. Once tamed, select it and press R to ride.
5. BUILDPress B, choose a structure with 1–4, then click to place it.
6. FARMBuild a Garden, press E to plant, wait, then press E again to harvest.
7. BREEDGet two adult tamed dinos of the same species close together and press N.
8. MULTIPLAYERHost a world and share the room code with your friends, or join theirs.
Important: the area around your starting camp is a safe zone. Predators cannot damage you there, and their attacks are no longer instant-kills.
